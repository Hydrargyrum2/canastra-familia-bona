Brazilian canasta (canastra) rules

Canastra família Bona is Brazilian canasta the way our family plays it. Two decks, runs and canastas, and the first side to 4,000 points wins. Below are the full rules the game uses, and you can try them right away: free in your browser, against bots or online with friends.

Other languages: Pravidla brazilské kanasty (canastra) · Regras da canastra

Play 1 vs 1, or four players in two teams. Two decks with 4 jokers, 13 cards each. Cards rank from 4 up to ace.

Melding

  • Runs of at least 3 cards in one suit, like 5♥ 6♥ 7♥.
  • Sets of the same rank only for kings, aces and fours.
  • Twos and jokers are wild, at most one per meld.
  • You can replace a wild card in a run with the card it stands for (from your hand or the pile). The wild card then moves to another spot in the same run, and you choose whether it goes to the bottom or the top. It can never go below 4 or above ace, and never to a different meld. If the run is complete from 4 to ace, the wild card goes at the end.
  • A wild card at the end of a run may move into a gap so that the card you add fits (from your hand or the pile). For example 7♦ 8♦ 2♦ (as 9♦) plus 5♦: the two moves to the 6♦ spot.
  • A canasta has 7 or more cards. A clean canasta has no wild card.
  • Above 2,000 points your first meld of a round must be worth at least 150. Everything you meld in that turn counts, even across several melds.
  • If you end the turn with less, the cards go back to your hand (a taken pile goes back on the table and you draw from the stock), you discard, and your team's minimum rises by 50 for the rest of the round (200, 250…).
  • Whatever you meld stays on the table. There is no Undo.

Your turn

  • Draw from the stock, or take the whole discard pile. You may take the pile only by melding its top card right away with cards from your hand (a wild card is fine), or by adding the top card alone to one of your melds. When adding, nothing comes from your hand; only a wild already in that meld may shift so the card fits.
  • A discarded black three, two or joker blocks the pile.
  • Red threes are laid down automatically and replaced with a new card.
  • You go out when you have no cards left and at least one canasta.
  • When the stock runs out, the next player may still play by taking the pile. Cards left in hand are then lost.

Starting a round

  • In the first round a coin toss decides who starts. After that the starting player changes every round (with four players it moves around the table).
  • The starting player cuts the deck. If exactly 26 cards stay at the bottom (52 with four players), their team gets 100 points straight away. Then 13 cards are dealt to each player.
  • Once per round, right after the first draw, the starting player may put the drawn card on the discard pile and draw another, as long as nothing has been melded yet. The turn then goes on as usual and ends with a discard.

Scoring

  • Cards 10, ace 20, two and joker 50.
  • Canasta 100. A clean canasta adds +100 for every card above 7.
  • Red three 100, but only if you have a canasta.
  • Whoever goes out gets a 100-point bonus plus the cards in the opponents’ hands. A black three there is worth 100.
  • First to 4,000 wins.

FAQ

How many players play canasta?
Two players head to head, or four players in two teams. In our game any seat can be taken by a bot.
How many cards are used?
Two 52-card decks plus four jokers (108 cards). Each player gets 13 cards.
How do you win at canasta?
Points are added after every round: cards on the table, canastas, red threes and, for whoever goes out, the cards left in the opponents’ hands. The game ends when someone reaches 4,000 points.
Is the game free?
Yes, it is completely free and ad-free. To play with friends you just create an account with a name and password.
